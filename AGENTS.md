# Research direction

The project investigates a verification layer for distinguishing human-created or human-captured content from AI-generated or AI-manipulated content, and for assessing human participation in interactions. Its scope includes meetings, posts, short videos, calls, messages and analogous uses across platforms, devices and communication channels. These are examples, not a closed list. Privacy, safety and reliability are central concerns.

The owner has moved beyond CAPTCHA as the solution direction. Keep CAPTCHA literature as historical background. Do not narrow the project to Internet traffic, financial fraud, or a particular transaction workflow without an explicit decision from the owner.

The canonical reading collection is [research/literature-review.md](research/literature-review.md). Keep concise, paraphrased source summaries with reliable original links and explicit publication status and limitations. Distinguish content origin, manipulation, provenance, human participation, identity and truthfulness; evidence for one does not establish the others. Treat architecture and implementation choices as undecided.
